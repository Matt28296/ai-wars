# Sigils

One mark per nation, plus ECHO's. Single-ink SVGs on a 24 px grid, exported at 96 px in each nation's fill colour (ECHO in `signal`, the Hollow Choir in its red signal `on-choir`). `<img>` can't recolour them; inside the game and the bundle, `Sigil` draws them live in any tone.

- `helion.svg` — rising chevron over a sunburst. Ink `helion` #f28c28.
- `tidewell.svg` — concentric ring with a centre dot. Ink `tidewell` #2f6fd8.
- `verdant.svg` — leaf triangle with a cut vein. Ink `verdant` #2ea36a.
- `kestrel.svg` — winged diamond. Ink `kestrel` #e6c95e.
- `choir.svg` — broken hexagon, struck through. Ink `on-choir` #ff4d63.
- `echo.svg` — four cursor brackets around a point: ECHO is the interface. Ink `signal` #5ce1ff.

Always pair a faction colour with its sigil; never use a sigil as decoration away from its nation.
