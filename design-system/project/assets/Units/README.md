# Units

Sixteen unit glyphs: single-ink filled silhouettes on a 24 px grid, drawn facing right, exported at 48 px in `ink` (#e8edf3) for dark grounds. On the battlefield `UnitToken` recolours them in the owner's `on-` token on the faction plate and mirrors them for left-facing units.

Ground: `trooper` · `breacher` · `skimmer` · `lancer` · `bastion` · `colossus` · `mule` · `arc` · `salvo` · `warden`. Air: `wasp` · `raptor` · `anvil` (top-down, symmetric). Naval: `picket` · `dreadnought` · `barge`.

Keep new glyphs to the same grid: 1.5–2 px minimum strokes, a clear silhouette at 24 px, weapon pointing right.
