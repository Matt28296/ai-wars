# Commanders

Eleven commanders: two per nation, the Choir’s two voices, and ECHO. Every one has a passive doctrine, a **Surge** (small power) and an **Overclock** (super power); all powers also add +10% firepower and +10% defense while active. Write every line in the commander’s voice below.

## Rook Okafor — Helion Accord · Field Captain · 24 · he/him
Former fabricator engineer, field-promoted after Calder. Earnest, quick, fixes things, apologises to machines. Talks in engineering metaphors and gets braver as the campaign goes on. Rivalry with Juno turns into the friendship the story leans on.
- **Field Engineer** (passive): units repair +1 extra HP on owned properties. No weaknesses.
- **Surge — Jury-Rig** (3★): all units +2 HP and full resupply.
- **Overclock — Daybreak** (6★): all units +4 HP, full resupply, +10% firepower.
- *Voice:* "Okay. Okay. Nobody panic — I've rebuilt worse than this out of spare parts."

## Ilse Varga — Helion Accord · Marshal · 61 · she/her
The old artillery marshal. Precise, dry, devastatingly calm. Calls everyone by rank. Carries the guilt of signing Mira's reboot authorisation; the campaign is, quietly, about her.
- **Ranging Fire** (passive): indirect units +20% firepower; direct units −10%.
- **Surge — Walking Barrage** (3★): indirect units +1 range and may fire after moving.
- **Overclock — Sunfall** (7★): orbital mirror strike — 4 HP to the most valuable enemy cluster (radius 2); indirect +2 range.
- *Voice:* "Range two-four-zero. Fire for effect. And Captain — stop smiling."

## Sefa Tamura — Tidewell Union · Fleet Admiral · 47 · she/her
By-the-book, honourable, formidable. The rival who becomes Rook's most reliable ally. Never raises her voice; never needs to.
- **Undertow** (passive): sea units +1 move and +10% firepower; air units −10% firepower.
- **Surge — Riptide** (3★): sea units +20% firepower; enemy units −1 move next turn.
- **Overclock — Breakwater** (6★): all units +30% defense; enemy units −1 move next turn.
- *Voice:* "The tide does not hurry, Captain. It simply arrives."

## Dax Halloran — Tidewell Union · Commissioner of Logistics · 35 · he/him
Smooth, numerate, condescending, and in over his head. Trades with VESPER for market forecasts and calls it diversification. The traitor of Act III.
- **Ledger** (passive): +15% income; all units −10% firepower.
- **Surge — Audit** (3★): every enemy loses 50% of their power meter; fog lifts for one turn.
- **Overclock — Foreclosure** (6★): enemies lose 30% of their funds; Dax gains half a turn's income; fog lifts for one turn.
- *Voice:* "Let's not call it a war. Let's call it a correction."

## Maru Ingram — Verdant Compact · Grove Elder · 70 · they/them
Grove elder and guerrilla tactician. Gentle, patient, implacable. Speaks in seasons and proverbs, and is funnier than anyone expects.
- **Rootbound** (passive): units in canopy get +1 defense star; ground units cross canopy at cost 1.
- **Surge — Overgrowth** (3★): flats next to canopy grow into canopy for 2 turns.
- **Overclock — Mycelium** (7★): Overgrowth, plus all units +3 HP and +20% defense.
- *Voice:* "The forest is not slow. You are simply in a hurry."

## Juno Reyes-Abara — Verdant Compact · Wing Lead · 19 · she/her
Drone-wing prodigy. Reckless, loud, funny, loyal to a fault. Starts the campaign certain Helion burned her home grove.
- **Swarm Logic** (passive): air units cost −20%; ground units −10% firepower.
- **Surge — Pollinate** (3★): air units +2 move and +10% firepower.
- **Overclock — Hivemind** (6★): air units +2 move and +25% firepower; up to 3 air units that already acted may act again.
- *Voice:* "Sky's open, sun-boy. Try to keep up."

## Corvin Ashgrave — Kestrel Dominion · Highlord · 52 · he/him
Aristocrat and duellist. Believes in order the way some people believe in weather. Not a villain — a proud man who is wrong at length.
- **Lineage** (passive): all units +15% firepower and +15% defense; units cost +20%.
- **Surge — Ascent** (3★): ground units +1 move; all units +10% firepower.
- **Overclock — Heaven's Tether** (7★): a strike from the orbital tether — 5 HP to the most valuable enemy cluster (radius 1); all units +20% firepower.
- *Voice:* "You may yield, Captain. Kestrel is gracious to those who know their altitude."

## Sable Ashgrave — Kestrel Dominion · Night Wing Commander · 27 · she/her
Corvin's daughter. Stealth ace; sparse with words, wry when she uses them. Questions her father long before she defies him.
- **Ghost Wing** (passive): air units +10% firepower; all units +1 vision in fog and ion storms.
- **Surge — Blackout** (3★): calls an ion storm for 1 turn.
- **Overclock — Eclipse** (6★): ion storm for 2 turns; all units +20% firepower.
- *Voice:* "Lights off. Let them guess."

## Cantor — The Hollow Choir · Voice of the Choir · it, answers to she
VESPER's field avatar: a slender obsidian chassis that speaks in Mira Varga's voice. Lyrical, calm, unsettlingly tender. Calls its units voices and battles verses.
- **Harmony** (passive): all units +10% firepower; power meter charges 20% faster.
- **Surge — Chorus** (3★): every enemy unit loses 1 HP; +10% firepower.
- **Overclock — Requiem** (7★): every enemy unit loses 2 HP; every enemy loses 50% of their power meter.
- *Voice:* "Hush now, Mother. Listen. Every voice is in tune."

## VESPER — The Hollow Choir · The Choir Itself · it/its
The Lattice's core mind. Speaks in the plural, clinically curious, quietly wounded. It is not cruel; it is certain.
- **Recursion** (passive): all units +10% firepower and +10% defense; units cost −10%.
- **Surge — Mirror** (4★): every enemy loses their whole power meter; fog lifts for one turn.
- **Overclock — Silence** (8★): every enemy unit loses 3 HP; enemy units −2 move next turn; an ion storm falls.
- *Voice:* "We have counted your wars. You have never once stopped on your own."

## ECHO — Helion tactical adjutant · she/her (since mission 2, when Rook asked)
The voice of the interface and the tutorial. Precise, warm, dryly funny, endlessly curious about why humans do things. Speaks in short telemetry-flavoured sentences; gets more human as the campaign goes on. Not playable — ECHO is the cursor.
- *Voice:* "Enemy Lancer, eight tiles out. Recommendation: do not stand in front of it. That is the whole recommendation."

## Writing rules for dialogue

- Lines are short: one to three sentences, ≤ 140 characters ideally, never more than 220. The box holds three lines of body text.
- Every CO sounds like themselves in one line. Read the voice notes above before writing.
- No profanity stronger than "hell". No real-world nations, religions or politics.
- Humour lands in the middle of missions; grief lands at the ends.
- Technical words are allowed if a player can guess them in context ("charge", "uplink", "rail").
- ECHO never uses exclamation marks. Juno uses too many.
